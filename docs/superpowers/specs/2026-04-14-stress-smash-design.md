# Stress Smash — Playful Mental Wellness Micro-App

**Date:** 2026-04-14  
**Status:** DESIGN APPROVED  
**Focus:** Tap-based stress relief game for burned-out office workers

---

## Problem

The burned-out office worker who can't meditate, doesn't have 5 minutes, and finds meditation apps too serious. They already use games for stress relief organically — but no product owns the 5-minute mobile format, and no product acknowledges their burnout context.

---

## Product Vision

A 5-minute tap-based stress release game that positions as a game, not a wellness app. Lead with the audience: "For people who've tried meditation and gave up." Owns the "stress relief game" keyword lane where Antistress lacks emotional depth and Betwixt is too slow.

---

## Core Concept

**Name:** Stress Smash (or Office Fists)  
**Format:** 5-minute tap/smash sessions  
**Position:** Game, not wellness  
**Target:** Burned-out office workers, ADHD/neurodivergent users who can't meditate

### The Moment

- **Open screen:** One line acknowledgment — *"Had a day? Let's punch something."*
- **Choose your stress target:** Angry inbox, deadline timer, blank spreadsheet, Monday morning
- **Punch for 2-5 minutes:** Tap to destroy/damage the target. Haptic + sound. Gradual destruction visual.
- **End screen:** One line send-off — *"You still hate your job, but your hands don't have to."*

No fail state. No timer by default (optional for speed-runners). No judgment.

---

## UX/UI Specification

### Visual Style
- **Aesthetic:** Playful, slightly irreverent, office-aware
- **Colors:** High contrast, energetic — not clinical blue/green
- **Motion:** Snappy, impact-focused — every tap lands visually + haptically

### Screens
1. **Open** — One-line empathy, tap to enter
2. **Target Select** — Grid of 4-6 stress targets with icons (inbox, deadline, spreadsheet, meeting, boss avatar, etc.)
3. **Play** — Main tap/smash canvas
4. **Complete** — Victory micro-animation, send-off message

---

## What NOT to Build

- Sleep stories (Headspace/Calm territory)
- Meditation guidance (self-defeating)
- Long narrative (Betwixt territory)
- Generic fidget without emotional layer (Antistress gap)
- Timer pressure by default

---

## Technical

- **Platform:** Flutter (iOS first, Android second)
- **No backend** — local state only initially
- **Analytics:** Track session length, target selection, return rate (no personal data)

---

## Positioning

- **Lead:** "For people who've tried meditation apps and given up."
- **Format promise:** "5 minutes. No sitting still. No failing at meditating."
- **Channel:** TikTok-first (not App Store SEO)
- **Language:** Office/burnout, not clinical

---

## Success Criteria

- Launch on TikTok with demo video
- 1,000 sessions within first week (validation)
- Target selection distribution signals audience preferences
- Return rate > 40%
